Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Information we collect

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information on our own servers. Wyrix Browser does not ask you to create an account.

Data stored on your device

Like any browser, the app stores browsing history, cookies, cached files and settings on your device so it can work properly. This data stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time from the app or your phone’s settings.

Websites you visit

When you open a website, that website may collect data under its own privacy policy. We do not control those websites.

Permissions

The app needs internet access to load websites. Any other permission, such as storage for downloads, is used only for the feature it belongs to.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this section.
