**Summary.** WYRIX has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and the developer does not receive your browsing data.

## Data stored on your device
Browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, downloads list, settings, saved articles, and (if you use it) the password vault. This data stays in the app's private storage and is excluded from Android cloud backup. Private tabs do not save history, and the app doesn't remember anything typed into them. The password vault is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore and never leaves your device. Clear data any time in Settings, or by uninstalling.

## Network activity
- **Web pages**: loaded directly from the sites you visit by the Mozilla Gecko engine. Those sites see your IP address and what any website sees. Tracking protection reduces third-party tracking.
- **Optional features you control**: search suggestions (sent to your selected search engine while you type, off by default, never in private tabs); page translation (opens Google Translate, which receives the page address); Reader mode (the app requests the page address itself without cookies); QR "open link" never loads anything until you confirm.
- **Downloads** use Android's download manager.

## Permissions
Camera (QR scanner, or when a website asks), microphone and location (only when a website asks and you allow it), biometric/screen-lock (optional app lock and password protection; handled by Android, the app never receives biometric data).

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
