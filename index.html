Wyrix Browser is a web browser. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask you to give us your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Browsing data such as history, bookmarks, cookies, cached files, saved logins, and site settings is stored on your device. We do not receive a copy of it.

2. Permissions the app may request

Permissions are requested only when a website or feature needs them, and you can deny or revoke them at any time in your device settings.

PermissionWhy it is used
Internet / network stateTo load web pages and detect whether you are online.
CameraWhen a website you visit asks to use your camera (for example, video calls or QR scanning), and only after you allow it.

Camera, microphone, and location data go to the website you allowed. They are not sent to us.

3. Websites you visit

When you open a website, your device connects to that site and its servers receive information such as your IP address and browser details. Those sites and their advertisers or analytics providers have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

5. How we use and share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for advertising. Any data that reaches us, such as an email you send to support, is used only to respond to you and to maintain and improve the app.

6. Data retention and your choices

Because browsing data stays on your device, you control it. You can clear history, cookies, and cache from the app’s settings, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and the data it handles, and the app uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections whenever a website supports them. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

Wyrix Browser is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
